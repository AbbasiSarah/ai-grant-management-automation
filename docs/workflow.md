
# Grant Management Workflow

## Overview
This document describes the proposed workflow for managing grant applications with AI assistance.

## Workflow Steps

### 1. Grant Planning
Maintain an annual plan of potential grant opportunities.

### 2. Grant Selection
Select suitable grants based on funding goals, eligibility, and deadlines.

### 3. Client Notification
Notify the client about relevant opportunities and their deadlines.

### 4. Application Review
Review the grant announcement and application requirements.

### 5. Client Information Collection
Identify the information and documents required from the client.

### 6. Requirement Extraction
Use an AI prompt to extract eligibility criteria, funding limits, deadlines, and required documents.

### 7. Grant Drafting
Prepare a draft using verified client information and grant requirements.

### 8. Fact-Checking
Check factual claims against reliable sources and supporting documents.

### 9. Peer Review
Ask a reviewer to check the draft for accuracy, clarity, and completeness.

### 10. Client Review
Share the draft with the client for corrections and approval.

### 11. Submission
Submit the application only after the client has approved it and the requirements have been checked.

### 12. Outcome and Follow-Up
Record the award or rejection, track reporting deadlines, and document follow-up actions.

## Human Oversight
AI-generated content must be reviewed by a person. Eligibility, financial figures, supporting evidence, and final submissions must be verified before use.

## Current Limitations
This is a practice project. The current version documents the workflow and tests AI prompts using fictional grant data. It does not yet automatically submit applications, contact clients, or retrieve live grant opportunities.
